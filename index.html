Privacy Policy

Last Updated · November 20, 2026

O-Next Keyboard respects user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles user data.

Data Collection

O-Next Keyboard does not collect, store, sell, or use personal data for advertising purposes.

The application does not collect or store personal information such as identity information, passwords, messages, contacts, location data, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage analytics, or any other personal or sensitive data on its own servers.

Keyboard Input

O-Next Keyboard may be used to type text into other applications. The application does not automatically record, store, analyze, or share the text you type with third parties.

Keyboard input is processed on your device and is sent only to the application or text field where you choose to type.

Voice Typing

O-Next Keyboard includes an offline voice typing feature. When you use it, your voice is processed entirely on your device using on-device speech recognition models — audio is never recorded, stored, or transmitted to any server, and is not shared with O-Next Keyboard or any third party.

To use voice typing in a given language, you may choose to download a speech recognition model for that language. This only downloads the model files themselves (not any personal data) from GitHub, where the models are hosted.

Translation Feature

When the translation feature in O-Next Keyboard is used, the text you choose to translate may be sent to the selected third-party translation service in order to perform the translation.

These services may include DeepL, Google Translate, or similar external translation providers.

The translation feature is used only when the user chooses to use it. O-Next Keyboard does not store, record, or keep the text sent for translation on its own servers. Text sent to third-party translation services may be processed according to the privacy policies of the relevant service providers.

GIF Search

O-Next Keyboard lets you search for and insert GIFs. When you use this feature, your search text is sent to a third-party GIF provider (Klipy) in order to return matching results.

This happens only when you choose to search for a GIF, and O-Next Keyboard does not store or keep your search queries on its own servers.

Clipboard History

O-Next Keyboard offers an optional clipboard history feature. If you use it, items you copy are stored locally on your device so you can reuse them later from the keyboard.

Clipboard history is never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with O-Next Keyboard or any third party, and stays on your device until you clear it.

Accessibility Service

O-Next Keyboard includes an optional accessibility service used solely to detect when a text field you were typing in becomes empty (for example, right after a message is sent), so the keyboard can correctly reset its suggestions.

This service does not read, record, store, or transmit the content of any text field.

Internet Access

O-Next Keyboard uses internet access only for online features. For example, the translation feature sends the text to be translated to the selected translation service, the GIF search feature sends your search text to the GIF provider to fetch results, and downloading an optional voice typing language model requires internet access.

Apart from this, the application does not transmit user data over the internet for advertising, analytics, tracking, or profiling purposes.

Third-Party Services

O-Next Keyboard does not use advertising networks, tracking systems, or analytics services that monitor user behavior.

However, when the translation feature is used, the text selected for translation may be sent to the chosen third-party translation service, and when the GIF search feature is used, your search text is sent to the GIF provider (Klipy). These processes are performed only to provide the relevant feature.

Children's Privacy

O-Next Keyboard does not knowingly collect personal data from users. The application does not intend to collect personal data from children.

Data Sharing

O-Next Keyboard does not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties for advertising purposes.

Only when the translation feature or GIF search feature is used, the relevant text may be technically transferred to the selected third-party service (translation provider or GIF provider) in order to deliver that feature.

Data Security

O-Next Keyboard does not store the text typed by users, voice audio, or clipboard history on its own servers. Text sent to third-party services as part of the translation or GIF search features is processed under the infrastructure and privacy policy of the relevant service provider.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page by updating the "Last Updated" date above.
